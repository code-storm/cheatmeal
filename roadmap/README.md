# CheatMeal — Year One Roadmap Index (Nov 2026 – Oct 2027)

> "The app that makes finishing today's pregnancy checklist feel like unlocking a limited drop — and makes you want to show someone."

That's CheatMeal's whole bet, stated once here so nothing else in this document set has to restate it. Everything below is about wiring that bet to real growth and retention over one year, in two halves.

## The two halves

**H1 (Nov 2026 – Apr 2027)** is already planned in detail: [VISION_ROADMAP_2026H1.md](../VISION_ROADMAP_2026H1.md). It diagnosed that CheatMeal's gamification core — points, levels, streaks, a cosmetic reward shop, badges, and licensed-IP crossover events like the "CheatMeal x Tokidoki" drop (Nov 15 – Dec 15, 2026) — was already built, and that the real gap was distribution and retention plumbing: analytics, a reminder channel, PWA installability, a referral loop, sharing, and Hindi localization. Six monthly phases work toward a **1,000+ WAU** target by April 2027.

**H2 (May 2027 – Oct 2027)** is this folder. It picks up exactly where H1 leaves off.

## Why H2 is planned in two tracks

H1 hasn't finished yet. Planning H2 today means committing to a single path before we know how H1 actually turned out — so instead, H2 is **dual-tracked**:

- **Track A — still solo/bootstrapped.** The default path. Assumes the operating reality hasn't changed: one contributor, free/near-free infrastructure, no paid acquisition budget. This is what's true today, so it's the safe baseline every month plans against.
- **Track B — if team/funding grows.** The stretch path. Only active if a real funding or hiring event has happened — not just because growth metrics look good. Track B should always read as "more hands make more possible," never as a different company with different goals.

Which track actually runs for H2 is **not a vibe decision**. It's determined by checking H1's real outcomes against explicit criteria in [01-decision-gate.md](01-decision-gate.md), checked once at the end of April 2027 (the H1/H2 boundary) and re-checked lightly at the end of July 2027 in case conditions change mid-H2.

**How to use this when you're actually there:** once H1 has really ended, read the gate file, classify where things landed, and then read *only* that track's column in each of the six month files below — Track A and Track B within a month are sequenced assuming the other track's prerequisites, so don't mix them.

## The three risks that don't expire at the H1/H2 boundary

H1 flagged three risks that stay live into H2 regardless of track — see [00-h1-recap.md](00-h1-recap.md) for detail:

- **Retention assumption** — H1's growth model assumes 30–45% retention; industry baseline is closer to 8%. Unvalidated until real analytics come in.
- **Branded-event sourcing** — the collectible crossover mechanic needs a new IP/brand partner every 6–8 weeks. That's a negotiation problem, not an engineering one, and nothing about H2 makes it easier.
- **Solo-founder bandwidth** — the real ceiling on scope, independent of how simple any single feature looks.

## The three H2 focus areas

Of H1's four deferred areas (no social network, no native app, no monetization, no new clinical domains), H2 activates three as real workstreams:

| Focus area | What it means | Primary months |
|---|---|---|
| **Light social** | Evolve H1's "Bump Squad" due-date cohorts into richer social proof — shareable recaps, trend views — while explicitly stopping short of a full social network (no DMs, no public profiles, no moderation burden) | May, Jun, Sep |
| **Native app evaluation** | A PWA→native decision *gate*, using H1's own analytics as evidence, not a committed rebuild — only becomes a real build on Track B | May, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct |
| **Content/domain expansion** | Extend beyond pregnancy into postpartum/new-parent content, the natural next lifecycle stage for retained users | May, Jun, Aug, Sep |

**Monetization is explicitly NOT a committed H2 workstream.** It stays deferred/exploratory at most — consistent with H1's own scope discipline — and is named as the obvious first candidate for H3 once a full year of real data exists.

## What H2 still won't do, either track

- **No full social network, forum, DMs, or public profiles.** Light social stops at read-only cohort aggregation, by design, in both tracks.
- **No committed native rebuild.** Native is an evaluation and a gate, not a build — unless Track B funding explicitly unlocks it, stated per-month.
- **No committed monetization build.** Exploratory signal-gathering only.
