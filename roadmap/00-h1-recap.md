## The three carried-forward risks

### Retention assumption
H1's growth model assumes D7-equivalent retention in the 30–45% range, well above the ~8% industry baseline — justified by CheatMeal's gamification already being live, but explicitly unvalidated until Phase 1's analytics produce a real number. Called out in H1 as "the single point of failure."

### Branded-event sourcing
The collectible/crossover mechanic depends on sourcing a new IP/brand partner every 6–8 weeks (Phase 3, Phase 5). This is a negotiation and relationship task, not an engineering one — H1 recommends starting outreach in parallel with other work, not sequentially.

### Solo-founder bandwidth
The real constraint on all scope, independent of any single feature's complexity. If Phase 4–6 work was already being cut to fit, H2 should plan for *less* throughput per month than H1's own cadence — not the same.

## What H1 explicitly deferred

H1's "will NOT do" list: no full social network/forum, no native iOS/Android app, no monetization work, no new clinical-content domains. This is exactly the set H2 is now revisiting, per the user's own decision:

- **Light social** (not a full social network) — activated in H2.
- **Native evaluation** (not a committed native app) — activated in H2 as an evidence-based gate.
- **Content expansion** (not a new clinical domain, but a new lifecycle stage — postpartum/new-parent) — activated in H2.
- **Monetization** — remains out of scope for H2 too, exploratory at most.
