## Track B — if team/funding grows

- Same native-evaluation analytics writeup, but with May's Capacitor/Expo spike as added input. If the spike went well and WAU/funding both support it, **this is the month Track B can greenlight an actual native build to start** — Play Store first, given the India-Android-dominant audience — explicitly gated on both (a) the spike having gone well and (b) funding still being real as of this re-check, not assumed from April.
- **If greenlit:** native build becomes the dominant Aug–Oct Track B workstream (see [05-aug-2027.md](05-aug-2027.md), [06-sep-2027.md](06-sep-2027.md), [07-oct-2027.md](07-oct-2027.md)), run in parallel with, not instead of, continued content/light-social work — Track B implies enough hands to parallelize.
